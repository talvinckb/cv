Dear Shift Technology team,

\justifying
I am a final-year engineering student at EPITA, specialized in computer vision and deep learning,
and I am applying for your Data \& AI Engineer internship. What draws me to this role is its scope:
owning one LLM feature from exploration to production, measured on precision, recall and latency.
In fraud detection, those numbers are not abstract. A false positive delays an honest customer, a
false negative lets a fraudulent claim through, and I want to learn to build AI under that kind of
constraint.

On the AI side, my final-year project with the National Library of France detects and classifies
illustrations in documents fetched through Gallica's IIIF API. I own the detection stage: training
on 10,000 annotated images, benchmarking models and tuning hyperparameters, up to 94.7\,\% mAP50.
I also implemented a U-Net from scratch in PyTorch, without any pretrained weights, to understand
models beyond their APIs. Both projects taught me that a model is only as good as the way it is
evaluated, which holds just as much for an agentic workflow as for a detector.

On the engineering side, I care about code quality and performance, in Python and beyond. At
Studiocanal, I shipped features in an object-oriented Java and Spring Boot codebase used by more
than 100 employees, and set up a GitLab CI/CD pipeline on AWS. As a freelancer, I build and operate
an inventory management application in production on my own, with backups, error tracking and
monitoring. In C++, I profiled a real-time video pipeline with Nsight and ported it to the GPU for a
24-fold speedup.

Finally, I work every day with coding agents such as Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity. Using them
has shown me how much an agent's output depends on its context, its tools and the checks around it.
I would now like to move from using agents to building them, and designing one with your Architects
and AI Research Lab, then deploying it with your forward-deployed Data Scientists, is exactly where
I want to learn to do that well.

I am available for a six-month internship in Paris from February 2027, and I would be glad to
discuss my application with you in an interview.
